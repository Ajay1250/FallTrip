Booking priorities

Book the trip anchors first. Everything else can stay weather-aware and flexible.

Book first

  • All three hotel stays
  • Acropole / Hautes-Gorges access and shuttle plan
  • Sentier des Cimes timed ticket
  • Tremblant gondola, once weather looks good
  • Ziptrek only if you choose it

Buy / check ahead

  • Mont-Mégantic park access
  • Le Massif lift / Le Buton access
  • Montagne Verte trail conditions and access
  • Fall hours, last entry and weather for every attraction

Leave open

  • Baie-Saint-Paul evening
  • Draveur Visitors Centre
  • Tremblant village and summit trails
  • Mont King / sunset viewpoints
  • Restaurants, unless there is a must-try spot

Accommodation strategy

Three bases, five nights, and only two hotel changes—enough comfort without adding inefficient morning drives.

Oct 7 · 1 night

Lac-Mégantic / Mont-Mégantic

A simple, clean overnight base after the longest drive. Prioritize easy check-in and parking over a resort stay.

Oct 8–9 · 2 nights

Baie-Saint-Paul

Your Charlevoix base for Hautes-Gorges, the town and the Le Massif departure. A motel-style stay is entirely suitable.

Oct 10–11 · 2 nights

Mont-Tremblant area

Compare pedestrian-village prices with hotels 10–20 minutes away. You have a car, so value matters more than a premium address.

What this trip gives you

Each stop earns its place by offering a different kind of fall experience—not another copy of the same hike.

⛰️

Big mountain days

Mont-Mégantic introduces the trip; Acropole des Draveurs is the serious hike; Le Buton adds a compact, high-payoff panorama.

🌊

Charlevoix scale

Hautes-Gorges brings river, cliffs and gorge scenery, while Baie-Saint-Paul creates room for an unhurried evening between ambitious days.

🌲

A new perspective

Sentier des Cimes is a canopy-level forest experience, distinct from the mountain summits and road views elsewhere on the route.

🌅

One sunrise

Montagne Verte supplies the trip’s single early start—an intentional highlight, rather than a daily habit.

🚠

Laurentian panorama

Tremblant’s gondola and summit trails provide large views without asking for one more punishing hike.

☕

Room to breathe

Oct 11 protects an adaptable leisure block for Ziptrek, a village afternoon, music, coffee or simply doing less.
