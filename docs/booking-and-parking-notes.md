# Booking and Parking Notes

## Parking
| Place | Parking |
|---|---|
| Microtel, Lac-Mégantic | On-site lot (confirm when booking) |
| Tanières | Lot near the reception pavilion, then a 5-minute walk with trolleys |
| Auberge Godard | Free on-site parking |
| Mont-Mégantic & Hautes-Gorges | Park lots; normally included with day access |
| Sentier des Cimes | About 300 spaces; parking included with the ticket |
| Montagne Verte | Trailhead lot only; street parking is banned |
| Fuel | Fill the tank on arrival in Baie-Saint-Paul; no fuel in Hautes-Gorges |

## Keep in mind
- Weekend parking at Sentier des Cimes may be busy, so arrive with enough time for the 4:30 PM slot.
- Montagne Verte requires a trailhead lot, not street parking.
- If the weather turns, the fallback for Day 3 is Mont du Lac-des-Cygnes in Parc national des Grands-Jardins.
