Day 2 note: The ASTROLab and observatory tours are not running on a weekday (the ASTROLab is open weekends in the fall), and public night observing is only offered during the July festival. This is a trails-and-views day.
Day 3 rules: - Trail season: the Acropole is normally open until the Monday of Thanksgiving weekend (Oct 12), subject to conditions. - Start rule: from Sept 3 until the season ends, you must start the trail by noon (sunrise to 12 PM). Turnaround at the last viewpoint is 3 PM from Oct 1. - On weekends and holidays a free shuttle from near the visitor centre is mandatory. On a weekday like Friday you can drive to the parking lot near the trailhead. If the gate is closed, ask the ranger about the shuttle and the walk-in. - Park entry is about $10.10 per adult (sepaq.com). Confirm the current rate. - Clouds can cover the summit. If it is socked in, wait about 30 minutes before deciding. - Use the washroom at the visitor centre or trailhead.
Rain / bad-weather fallback for Oct 9: If the forecast is poor, do Mont du Lac-des-Cygnes in Parc national des Grands-Jardins instead (8.6 km round trip, half day, about 30 km up Route 381 from Baie-Saint-Paul to the visitor centre). Daily park access is capped, so reserve on sepaq.com. Valley trails and the Draveur visitor centre in Hautes-Gorges are the other easy option.
Day 4 note: Sunset plan: weekend tickets are limited by time slot, and the sunset slots sell out first, so book the 4:30 PM entry today. Last admission is one hour before closing. Confirm the Oct 10 closing time on the official hours page. Closing at 6:30 PM or later is what makes a sunset visit work. If it closes at 6 PM, choose the earliest afternoon slot you can and go straight to the tower. Cloud can hide the sunset, and the Laurentians may be just past peak colour, so treat the golden-hour light as the main payoff. If you are running late, skip the Route 362 loop or shorten the lunch stop so you can still make the 4:30 PM entry.

Before you go

Weather and daylight

Check the Oct 9 hike forecast on Oct 8 evening and recheck trail conditions in the morning. Sunrise is about 7:10 AM; sunset is around 5:50–6:00 PM. Carry a headlamp.

Trail safety

Acropole conditions can change; use the valley-trail fallback if rain or weather makes the summit unsafe. Wear bright colours on trails outside the major parks during October hunting season.

Pack for the morning

Bring warm layers, gloves, rain jackets and hiking shoes. It can be near freezing at the Montagne Verte sunrise.

Offline navigation

Cell service is weak in Hautes-Gorges and parts of the Appalachian region. Download offline maps for the full route before leaving.

Long-weekend traffic

Thanksgiving is Monday Oct 12, making Oct 10–11 a busy long weekend. Check live traffic before leaving Nominingue on Sunday and keep the 5:30–5:45 AM departure in mind.

Payments and estimates

Bring a credit card and some cash for small shops and parking. The trip budget is in CAD for two adults; hotel, fuel and admission figures are estimates, not live quotes.

Confirm before departure: Lac-Mégantic hotel booked and midnight check-in agreed (Oct 6 call); Mont-Mégantic summit-road access and trail status; Hautes-Gorges visitor-centre opening time and gate-code queue; Sentier des Cimes Oct 10 closing time and the 4:30 PM slot; Auberge Godard dinner, late arrival, and key drop; Montagne Verte trailhead access and any fee; road work and lookout access on Route 138, Route 362, and Route 117.
